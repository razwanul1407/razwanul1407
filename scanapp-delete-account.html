What Gets Deleted

When your request is processed, the following are permanently erased from our systems:

  • Firebase Authentication record: your login identity, including email/password credentials or the linked Google Sign-In account.
  • User profile: user ID, email address, display name, profile photo URL, sign-in provider, account creation and last-login timestamps.
  • Push notification token: the device token used to deliver notifications to you.
  • Any cloud-synced app data associated with your account in our Firebase Realtime Database.
Data Retention & What Is Not Affected
  • Documents on your device: scanned PDFs and images are stored locally on your phone and are never uploaded. Deleting your account does not remove them — delete them from the app or uninstall MeScan.
  • Anonymous crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics retains anonymised crash logs for up to 90 days. These contain no personal identifiers and are automatically purged.
  • Deletion request record: we retain a record of your request (email address and date) for up to 90 days to prove compliance, then delete it.
  • No data is sold, shared, or retained for marketing after deletion.
How the Process Works
  • Step 1 — You submit the form above with your name, account email and reason.
  • Step 2 — We verify the email address matches an existing MeScan account.
  • Step 3 — Your account and all associated cloud data are permanently deleted within 7 days of the request.
  • Step 4 — A confirmation email is sent to you once the deletion is complete.

You can also delete your account directly from the app: open Profile → Settings and choose Delete Account. If you no longer have access to the app, use this form instead.
